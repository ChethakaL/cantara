CANTARA — AGENT INPUTS AND ACCEPTED FILE TYPES
Code audit date: 7 October 2026

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This is a read-only audit of the code in cantara-next. It lists the client/advisor
inputs for the agents, generators and calculators, plus the file formats offered
by the client portal and the relevant advisor screens. It is not a live-production
upload test. Per-client assignments and database-configured questions can vary.
No code was changed for this audit.

HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT
- "Portal" means the shared Document Upload rules, used by both the portal picker
  and the client-documents/upload API. Advisor screens may have different rules.
- "Advisor" means the dedicated agent screen, not the client upload checklist.
- "No restriction configured" means the portal and upload API do not enforce a
  file-type rule for that slot. It does NOT prove the agent can analyze every type.
- Listed documents are inputs to collect, not necessarily all hard requirements.
  Explicit run requirements are called out where verified.
- JPG and JPEG are the same image format (.jpg and .jpeg).
- PDF/image portal slots offer .pdf, .png, .jpg, .jpeg and .webp. Several legal,
  employment and tax analyzers explicitly recognize only PDF, PNG and JPEG as
  document/image attachments. Use PDF, PNG or JPEG for those agents; WEBP is a
  portal upload option, but is not consistently handled by their analysis routes.
- DOC and DOCX are different formats. Several screens allow old .doc files, but
  use a DOCX extractor or have no dedicated .doc parser. Prefer .docx or PDF.
- MP3, WAV, MP4, HEIC, ZIP and GIF are not general supported portal formats.
  Specific discrepancies and exceptions are recorded near the end.

A. FINANCIAL / VALUATION

1. Valuation Agent (ttm; contains six valuation sub-agents)
Inputs:
  - Required for the initial financial analysis: 36 months of monthly profit and
    loss statements and 36 months of monthly balance sheets, with GL/account
    codes and account names where available.
  - Fiscal year-end information from Required Info.
  - Optional: three fiscal years of accountant-prepared financial statements.
  - Recast inputs: personal expenses and material non-recurring expenses for
    36 months; advisor add-back/replacement compensation assumptions.
  - Legacy/supporting inputs: shareholder remuneration, tenant improvements,
    lease, Owner & GM Assessment output, additional valuation context.
Portal formats, by input:
  - Monthly P&L: PDF, XLSX, XLS.
  - Monthly balance sheet: PDF, XLSX, XLS.
  - Accountant statements: PDF, XLSX, XLS.
  - Shareholder remuneration, personal expenses, non-recurring expenses:
    CSV, XLSX, XLS.
  - Tenant improvements: PDF, CSV, XLSX, XLS.
  - Additional valuation context: PDF, XLSX, XLS (advisor-only context slot).
Important:
  The active monthly-statement resolver supports Excel/Excel or PDF/PDF pairs.
  Excel is preferred when both Excel and PDF are present in a slot. Do not mix
  a PDF P&L with an Excel balance sheet. CSV is mentioned in older advisor slot
  definitions, but is neither offered by the shared monthly upload rules nor
  classified as Excel by the current monthly-statement resolver.
  Accountant statement help text mentions ZIP, but the shared upload rules do
  not accept ZIP. Upload a merged PDF or individual year files instead.
  The initial analysis requires only P&L and balance sheet; the older advisor
  array's "required" label on add-back files is not the API's hard requirement.
Sources: src/lib/ttm-agent/types.ts; src/lib/ttm-agent/orchestrator.ts;
  src/lib/ttm-agent/pdf-monthly-statements.ts;
  src/components/ttm-agent/TtmAnalysisTab.tsx; shared upload rules.

Valuation sub-agents (inside the Valuation Agent; not six extra portal uploads):
  a. WS2-1 Financial Analysis: monthly P&L + balance sheet; optional accountant
     statements/context; creates the annual/TTM financial model.
  b. WS2-2 Recast / EBITDA Normalization: initial financial model, advisor
     normalization assumptions, optional personal/non-recurring expense files;
     legacy disclosures and Owner & GM/lease/context evidence where supplied.
  c. WS2-3 Revenue by Vertical: approved initial model and available recast data;
     derives revenue categories/service-line trends. No separate required file.
  d. WS2-4 Expense Benchmarking: approved initial model, available recast data
     and built-in benchmarks. No separate required file.
  e. WS2-5 Labor Expense Analysis: approved initial model and completed recast;
     replacement compensation and optional Owner & GM assessment context.
  f. WS2-10 Financial Report Generator: approved recast and completed WS2-3,
     WS2-4 and WS2-5 outputs. No separate required client document.
  Downstream runs have advisor approval/release gates in the orchestrator.
Sources: src/app/api/ttm-agent/{orchestrate,recast,derived}/route.ts;
  src/lib/ttm-agent/orchestrator.ts; ws3-revenue.ts; ws4-benchmarks.ts; ws5-labor.ts.

2. External Valuation Report (alternative input workflow, not a separate catalog agent)
Input: an externally prepared business valuation report.
Dedicated advisor upload: PDF, XLSX, XLS, CSV, DOCX; maximum 25 MB.
This is distinct from the narrower "Additional Valuation Context" document slot.
The roadmap/buyer report can select the stored external valuation as its source.
Sources: src/components/ttm-agent/ExternalValuationReport.tsx;
  src/app/api/ttm-agent/external-report/route.ts.

B. RISK, LEGAL AND OPERATIONS

3. Employee Obligations Agent (employee_obligations)
Inputs:
  - Key employee/manager contracts, bonus agreements and amendments.
  - Non-compete/non-solicitation agreements; current employee handbook.
  - Benefits summary; independent contractor agreements and 1099 records.
  - Management offer letters; severance/separation agreements (last 24 months).
  - Retirement plan documents; current PTO balances/accrual ledger.
  - Workers' compensation claims (last 24 months).
  - Client name, state, DBA and optional employee count/employment breakdown.
Run requirement: at least one document and client name in the analysis API.
Portal formats:
  - Key employee contracts: PDF only.
  - Contractor/1099 slot: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP (prefer PDF/PNG/JPEG).
  - Handbook, non-competes, benefits, offer letters, severance, retirement, PTO
    and workers' compensation slots: no file-type restriction configured.
Advisor picker formats:
  - Contracts, non-competes, handbook, offers, severance, retirement: PDF, DOCX, DOC.
  - Benefits, contractors and workers' compensation: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, DOC.
  - PTO ledger: XLSX, XLS, CSV, PDF.
Analysis caveat: explicit binary handling exists for PDF, PNG/JPEG, XLSX and DOCX;
  legacy DOC/XLS are not equivalently parsed. Use PDF/DOCX for text and XLSX for
  spreadsheet evidence. Formats shown by a picker do not guarantee parsing.
Sources: src/components/ws1-6/WS16Uploader.tsx;
  src/app/api/employee-obligations/analyze/route.ts; shared upload rules.

4. Employee Staffing & Compensation Agent (employee_comp)
Inputs: employee/payroll roster with names, titles, weekly hours, compensation,
  location, employment type, hire/rehire dates, pay basis, effective dates and
  benefits classifications where available. No SSNs. Also uses the 24-hour-care
  answer as business operating context. Supports pasted text and manual rows.
Portal employee-list format: XLSX, XLS only.
Advisor direct analysis: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, XLSX, XLS, CSV; pasted text/manual entry.
Sources: src/components/employee-comp/EmployeeCompTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/employee-comp/analyze/route.ts; src/lib/employee-comp/analyze.ts.

5. Insurance Review Agent (insurance_review)
Inputs: all active business insurance policies; claim documents from the last
  12 months if claims occurred. Policy and claim files are read from stored uploads.
Portal and advisor formats: PDF only for both policies and claims.
The analysis API needs at least one stored insurance file; claims are conditional.
Sources: src/components/admin/InsuranceReviewTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/insurance-review/route.ts; shared upload rules.

6. Lease Analysis Agent (lease_analysis)
Inputs: base lease(s), every amendment/addendum/rider; client/property context.
Portal and advisor formats: PDF only.
Text-searchable PDFs are preferred. The route also has handling for scanned or
  low-text PDFs with an upstream page limit; large scanned packs may need splitting.
Property owners see lease requests only when the advisor enables lease analysis.
Sources: src/components/lease-analysis/LeaseUploader.tsx;
  src/app/api/lease-analysis/analyze/route.ts; src/lib/client-portal-documents.ts.

7. Legal Reports & Entity Search Agent (legal_entity_search)
Actual advisor-screen inputs:
  - Secretary of State filings (articles/formation records, amendments, annual reports).
  - Certificates of good standing/existence.
  - Registered agent confirmations.
  - Corporate governance records (operating agreement, bylaws, shareholder
    agreement, resolutions): supplemental.
  - Trademark/IP registrations and assignments: supplemental.
  - UCC search results, UCC-1/continuation filings and secured debt schedules:
    direct advisor input; required when "Advisor to Run" is active.
  - Business name, state, DBA, entity type and business address context.
Portal formats for the linked legal documents: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP;
  prefer PDF/PNG/JPEG for analysis.
Advisor direct picker: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, JPG, JPEG.
Important mapping differences:
  UCC search results and registered agent confirmations have no dedicated client
  portal document slot. The advisor uploads them on this agent screen.
  The catalog mapping includes business licenses and ownership/cap table, but
  the actual Legal screen's input categories/import mapping are the categories
  above. Do not substitute the catalog list for the actual input screen.
  The advisor screen blocks a run when SOS/good-standing/registered-agent
  categories are missing (unless its advisor-run mode changes that gate). The
  API itself checks documents/client name, rather than every category.
Sources: src/components/legal-entity-search/LegalEntitySearchTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/legal-entity-search/analyze/route.ts.

8. Litigation & Liens Agent (litigation_search)
Inputs: business name and state (required for public-record search), owner name,
  optional county/city; formal lien/court/title records and pending-litigation
  disclosures as documentary evidence.
Portal pending-litigation format: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP.
Lien-search/court-record slot: same shared formats, but hidden from client portal;
  Cantara sources/handles these records.
Advisor direct upload: PDF or image (picker uses image/*).
Public-record search can run without a document upload.
Sources: src/components/litigation-search/LitigationSearchTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/litigation-search/route.ts; src/lib/litigation-search/search.ts;
  src/lib/client-portal-documents.ts.

9. Material Contracts Agent (contract_analysis)
Inputs: vendor, supplier, software and service agreements over $5,000/year OR
  lasting longer than 12 months, including amendments. Portal instructions ask
  for an agreement-details spreadsheet where no formal contract exists.
Portal slot: PDF, XLSX, XLS.
Advisor direct picker and current analysis route: PDF.
Critical mismatch: the analysis route treats every attachment as PDF (or extracts
  PDF text). A spreadsheet can be stored in the portal, but there is no matching
  Excel parser in this agent route. For analysis, supply a PDF of those details.
Sources: src/lib/documentData.ts; src/components/contract-analysis/ContractUploader.tsx;
  src/app/api/contract-analysis/analyze/route.ts.

10. Org Chart Review Agent (org_chart_review)
Input: current organization chart with names, roles/departments, reporting lines,
  headcount and key-person/transition information.
Portal format: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP.
Advisor picker: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, XLSX, XLS, CSV.
Analysis: handles PDF/images; spreadsheet files are passed as native document
  blocks rather than parsed locally, so provider support may differ. PDF/PNG/JPEG
  are the clearest choices for an organization diagram.
Sources: src/components/org-chart/OrgChartReviewTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/org-chart/analyze/route.ts; src/lib/org-chart/analyze.ts.

11. Owner & GM Assessment Agent (owner_gm_assessment)
Actual analysis input: owner/GM interview, discovery-call transcript or meeting
  notes describing involvement, responsibilities, hours, compensation, GM role,
  succession/transition and bench strength.
Advisor picker: PDF, TXT, DOCX, DOC. Prefer PDF/TXT/DOCX; legacy DOC extraction
  uses the same Word extractor and should not be promised as reliable.
Portal catalog-associated supporting uploads:
  - Employee list: XLSX, XLS.
  - Org chart: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP.
  - SOP manual: PDF, DOCX, DOC.
Important: the current initial-analysis endpoint uses the uploaded transcript
  file. The catalog's employee list/org chart/SOP association is not proof that
  those three files are automatically consumed in this analysis run.
Sources: src/components/owner-gm-assessment/OwnerGmAssessmentTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/owner-gm-assessment/route.ts; src/lib/owner-gm-assessment/analyze.ts.

12. Ownership Verification Agent (ownership_verification)
Inputs: articles of organization/incorporation; shareholders agreement if any;
  current operating agreement/bylaws; organizational amendments; certificates
  of good standing; most recent annual reports; name/state/DBA/entity type.
Portal formats for all six document groups: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP.
Advisor direct picker: PDF, DOCX, DOC.
Analyzer has explicit handling for PDF, PNG/JPEG, DOCX and XLSX. Legacy DOC and
  WEBP are not equivalently handled. Prefer PDF/PNG/JPEG for portal documents.
API allows a documented "all documents unavailable" path, rather than requiring
  every listed document in every case.
Sources: src/components/ws1-8/WS18Uploader.tsx;
  src/app/api/ownership-verification/analyze/route.ts; src/lib/workstream-agents.ts.

13. Permits & Zoning Agent (permits_zoning)
Inputs: business licenses; kennel license; health permit; fire permit; zoning
  verification/approval; certificate of occupancy; conditional/special-use
  permit; signage permits; building permits; environmental permits; variance
  approvals. Client name, state, DBA, property address and municipality provide context.
Portal formats: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP for all these document slots.
Advisor direct picker: PDF, DOCX, DOC for every slot.
Analyzer explicitly handles PDF, PNG/JPEG, DOCX and XLSX. Prefer PDF/PNG/JPEG
  through the portal; DOC and WEBP are not equivalently handled.
API also supports an "all documents unavailable" path.
Sources: src/components/ws1-9/WS19Uploader.tsx;
  src/app/api/permits-zoning/analyze/route.ts; src/lib/workstream-agents.ts.

14. Real Estate Appraisal Agent (real_estate_appraisal)
Input: one current appraisal document for property owned by the business.
Portal: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP.
Advisor picker: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG.
Analysis route accepts PDF or an image MIME type. Portal request is shown for
  owners when appraisal is enabled, and hidden for leaseholders.
Sources: src/components/admin/RealEstateAppraisalTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/real-estate-appraisal/analyze/route.ts; client-portal-documents.ts.

15. Software & Vendors Agent (vendor_directory)
Inputs: manually entered vendor/software list: product/service name, vendor,
  category, annual cost, contract status, transferability, login-access ownership
  and notes. Contract analysis can populate vendor records.
File types: no dedicated upload input for this directory. Contract evidence is
  collected through Material Contracts, with the limitations listed there.
Sources: src/components/vendor-directory/VendorDirectoryTab.tsx;
  src/lib/contract-analysis/merge-vendors.ts; contract analysis routes.

16. Tax Liability Review Agent (tax_liability_review)
Inputs:
  Required document groups on the agent screen:
    - Federal/state income tax returns for the last 3 years.
    - IRS Forms 941 and 940 for the last 3 years.
    - State unemployment (SUI/SUTA) filings for the last 3 years.
    - State sales/use tax returns, schedules and payment records for 3 years.
  Supplemental: 1099-NEC/1099-MISC and contractor agreements; IRS/state tax
    notices, audit correspondence, deficiency letters for the last 3 years.
  Context: client name, state, entity type, fiscal year-end, employee count.
Portal:
  - All listed tax groups except state unemployment: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP.
  - State unemployment: no file-type restriction configured.
Advisor picker: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, DOC, PNG, JPG/JPEG.
Use PDF/PNG/JPEG from the portal, or PDF/DOCX/XLSX directly with the advisor.
Old DOC and WEBP have no equivalent explicit parser/image branch in this route.
Multi-year income-tax uploads allow a combined file or individual year files;
  ZIP is advertised in description text but is rejected by shared upload rules.
Sources: src/lib/tax-readiness.ts; src/components/tax-liability-review/TaxLiabilityReviewTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/tax-liability-review/analyze/route.ts; shared upload rules.

C. MARKET, PERFORMANCE AND GROWTH

17. Client Location Map Agent (client_location_map)
Inputs: customer/client address spreadsheet with customer names, street address,
  city, state, ZIP/postcode and country; optional service type (boarding, daycare,
  grooming). Facility address is used as the map center. Missing service type
  defaults to Both / Multiple. A Cantara address-list template is available.
Portal/advisor document input: CSV, XLSX, XLS.
PDF/JPEG are not the portal formats for this customer address list.
Sources: src/components/client-location-map/ClientLocationMapTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/client-location-map/route.ts; src/lib/client-portal-documents.ts.

18. Competitor Analysis Agent (competitor_analysis)
Inputs: business name and physical address (required by API), business category,
  website, radius, named competitors and optional competitor addresses/websites.
The portal defines five competitor slots, with name/website required fields and
  address/category optional. Analysis can also discover businesses via Places.
File types: no dedicated document upload is required. Inputs are form text/URLs
  plus public Google Places and website research.
Sources: src/lib/competitor-analysis/types.ts; src/lib/competitor-form-fields.ts;
  src/app/api/competitor-analysis/analyze/route.ts.

19. Competitive Pricing Analysis Agent (pricing_analysis)
Actual run inputs: seller website URL and exactly FIVE competitor names and
  website URLs; optional pasted/manual seller and competitor pricing evidence.
Also uses saved Competitor Analysis pricing findings/website research.
Portal-associated documents: current/prior pricing schedules and revenue
  breakdown. Each allows PDF, CSV, XLSX, XLS.
Important: the current analysis route runs from URLs, manual evidence and saved
  competitor data; it does not directly parse these two portal documents.
File types for the core run: none; provide URLs and text. Those portal documents
  remain supporting collection, not a substitute for the five competitor inputs.
Sources: src/app/api/pricing-analysis/route.ts;
  src/components/pricing-analysis/PricingAnalysisTab.tsx; shared upload rules.

20. Digital Presence Agent (digital_presence)
Inputs: business name; website URL; Google Business Profile URL; Facebook,
  Instagram and TikTok handles/URLs; booking platform; Yelp, Nextdoor, LinkedIn,
  Glassdoor and BBB URLs. The Required Info route currently hides the separate
  Google Business locations question.
File types: no dedicated required upload. Uses form text/URLs and online research.
The legacy online-review document slot has PDF/image rules but is hidden from
  client uploads; it is not a required input to the current digital analysis API.
Sources: src/lib/digital-presence/types.ts;
  src/app/api/digital-presence/analyze/route.ts;
  src/app/api/client-form-questions/route.ts.

21. Facility Review Agent (facility_review)
Client/standard mode inputs: business/location details, condition notes and
  photos. Portal photo sections: exterior, reception, boarding, grooming, indoor
  play, outdoor play, and staff/operational areas.
Photos: JPG/JPEG, PNG, WEBP. Portal allows up to five images per photo section;
  standard analysis API allows up to 30 images, maximum 5 MB per image.
Supporting portal document slots: health/safety inspection reports and violations
  or citations for the last 36 months: PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG, WEBP.
Advisor visit mode: pasted meeting/visit notes OR a notes document; optional
  photos and supporting documents. Notes/supporting documents: PDF, DOCX, TXT.
Limits in advisor analysis: 20 photos, 5 MB each; up to 8 supporting documents,
  15 MB each; notes file maximum 15 MB. Notes are required in advisor mode.
Important: the main standard analysis route consumes images and notes. Health/
  safety and violation checklist uploads are associated evidence, not proof that
  this route automatically reads those files.
Sources: src/app/dashboard/page.tsx (facility photo intake);
  src/components/facility-review/FacilityReviewTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/facility-review/{analyze,advisor-analyze,extract-notes}/route.ts.

22. Occupancy Review Agent (occupancy_review)
Inputs: 24 months of monthly boarding/daycare counts; owner-stated total daily
  capacity, boarding runs/kennels, daycare spots; optional grooming and bathing
  stations and analysis period. Monthly data can also be entered/reviewed in forms.
Portal main data slot: CSV, XLSX, XLS. A sample CSV template is available.
Advisor main-slot picker also offers PDF, but upload uses the shared API, which
  rejects PDF for occupancy_review.
Advisor supporting slot (occupancy_supporting): picker offers PDF, XLSX, CSV,
  XLS, DOCX, PNG, JPG/JPEG; no shared per-slot format rule is configured.
Analysis caveat: PDF is attached as PDF; other file bytes are decoded as UTF-8 in
  the analysis route. Main portal CSV/Excel uploads have a separate XLSX-based
  parser that populates structured monthly/capacity inputs. Use those structured
  inputs and verify the review grid. Direct binary XLSX/DOCX/image supporting
  files are not reliably analyzed merely because the picker accepts them.
Sources: src/components/occupancy-review/OccupancyReviewTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/occupancy-review/route.ts; src/lib/occupancy-upload-parser.ts;
  src/lib/occupancy-form-fields.ts.

23. Pricing by Vertical Agent (pricing_vertical)
Inputs: current pricing and changes over the last 24 months; historical rate
  cards/schedules; revenue breakdown by service line (36 months/three fiscal years
  and TTM); business website; saved WS2-3 Revenue by Vertical output when present.
Portal pricing schedule and revenue breakdown: PDF, CSV, XLSX, XLS.
Advisor picker:
  - Pricing schedule: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, XLS, CSV, PNG, JPG/JPEG.
  - Revenue breakdown: XLSX, XLS, CSV, PDF.
Uploads through the shared document API remain restricted to the portal formats;
  DOCX/images offered by the pricing picker are rejected for pricing_schedule.
The selected stored-document workflow extracts spreadsheet text and supplies
  native PDF/image attachments. Text-based PDF/CSV/XLSX/XLS evidence is supported
  by the document evidence loader; do not assume every legacy single-file input
  has the same parsing behavior.
Sources: src/components/pricing-vertical/PricingByVerticalTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/pricing-vertical/route.ts;
  src/lib/pricing-vertical/document-evidence.ts; analyze.ts.

24. Sales Process Review Agent (sales_process_review)
Actual run input: discovery/sales/customer inquiry call transcript or written
  conversation notes, uploaded by Cantara/advisor. Business name is context.
Transcript formats: PDF, TXT, DOCX, DOC. Prefer text-readable PDF/TXT/DOCX;
  legacy DOC uses a DOCX extractor, so support is not reliable.
The sales_process_transcript slot is hidden from client portal Document Upload.
No MP3/WAV/MP4 transcription upload is implemented in this agent route, despite
  some labels mentioning a "recording". Upload a written transcript.
Catalog also associates pricing_schedule (PDF, CSV, XLSX, XLS), but the actual
  analysis endpoint loads the latest sales_process_transcript, not the pricing file.
Sources: src/components/sales-review/SalesProcessReviewTab.tsx;
  src/app/api/sales-review/analyze/route.ts; src/lib/sales-review/analyze.ts;
  src/lib/client-portal-documents.ts.

D. REPORTS, DEAL PROCESS AND MANUAL DIRECTORIES

25. Sales Readiness Roadmap (sales_readiness_roadmap)
Inputs: completed diligence agent outputs; advisor-selected source exclusions;
  advisor-edited checklist category/item/status/action wording and approvals.
Checklist generation needs at least one eligible completed source (or selected
  external valuation input); full-report generation uses approved checklist items.
Direct file types: none. It synthesizes saved outputs. External valuation can be
  selected; see entry 2 for the dedicated upload formats.
Legacy WS1/WS2 roadmap aliases resolve to this unified roadmap agent.
Sources: src/app/api/improvement-roadmap/route.ts;
  src/lib/completed-agent-outputs.ts; src/lib/workstream-agents.ts.

26. WS1 Assessment Report (ws1_assessment)
Inputs: stored WS1 legal, compliance, operational and financial agent findings
  and client details. No dedicated source document upload.
File types: none directly; uses saved agent reports.
Status: still present in catalog/API, but the standard internal-assessment tab
  is commented out in the current advisor navigation.
Sources: src/app/api/assessment-report/route.ts;
  src/components/assessment-report/AssessmentReportTab.tsx;
  src/app/admin/client/[id]/page.tsx.

27. WS2 Assessment Report (ws2_assessment)
Inputs: stored WS2 financial, market, growth and operations agent findings and
  client details. No dedicated source document upload.
File types: none directly; uses saved agent reports.
Status: same catalog/API availability and commented-out navigation as WS1.
Sources: same files as entry 26, using workstream=ws2.

28. WS1 / WS2 Buyer Reports (buyer_report)
Inputs: a generated full Sales Readiness Roadmap (required by API), saved
  workstream agent outputs and optional selected external valuation.
File types: none directly. External valuation formats are in entry 2.
Two advisor report tabs use the same API with different workstream values.
Sources: src/app/api/buyer-report/route.ts;
  src/components/buyer-report/BuyerReportTab.tsx.

29. CIM Generator Agent (cim)
Inputs: client/business description and location, deal/transaction terms,
  ownership/management, operations, staffing, property/lease details, financial
  history and TTM, normalization, service lines, competitive/digital information,
  risks, growth initiatives and contacts. Advisor can edit the structured CIM form.
Auto-fill reads client profile and available valuation/recast, lease, competitor,
  digital presence, employee obligations and insurance review outputs.
File types: no required new diligence upload; uses existing reports and form text.
The CIM editor also offers optional image uploads (image/*) for visual assets;
  these are presentation assets, not required analysis documents.
Sources: src/lib/cim/types.ts; src/app/api/cim/auto-fill/route.ts;
  src/components/cim/CimGeneratorTab.tsx.

30. Deal Teaser Generator Agent (teaser)
Inputs: transaction snapshot, region/location, business/service model, capacity,
  financial highlights (revenue, growth, normalized EBITDA, margin), investment
  highlights, operating/property details, contact information and branding.
Auto-fill uses client profile and available valuation/recast, lease, competitor,
  employee obligations, insurance and digital presence reports.
File types: no dedicated required upload; structured text and saved reports.
Sources: src/lib/teaser/types.ts; src/app/api/teaser/auto-fill/route.ts;
  src/components/teaser/TeaserGeneratorTab.tsx.

31. LOI Review & Comparison (loi_review)
Inputs: one or more buyer letters of intent/offers; client details for comparison
  of price, structure, working capital, earnout/holdback, diligence, financing,
  timing, closing conditions, exclusivity and other terms.
Advisor picker: PDF, DOCX.
Analysis caveat: PDF is attached properly, but all non-PDF files are decoded as
  raw UTF-8 without a Word extractor in this route. DOCX is advertised but not
  properly parsed here. Use PDF for the current comparative analysis.
The separate prior_offers portal checklist slot accepts PDF, DOCX, DOC; that does
  not establish equivalent parsing or automatic import into the LOI analysis.
Sources: src/components/loi-review/LoiReviewTab.tsx;

E. AUDIT FINDINGS TO KEEP THE REQUEST LIST ACCURATE

1. No single consistent "all agents accept PDF/JPEG/Excel" rule exists. Formats
   are controlled by document slot and upload path.
2. Ten defined checklist slots have no shared format rule: state unemployment,
   employee handbook, non-competes, benefits, offer letters, severance, retirement,
   PTO ledger, workers' compensation and the hidden/unused customer-count slot.
   This is a validation gap, not verified support for arbitrary formats.
3. PDF/image slot pickers include WEBP, despite their hint saying only PNG/JPG.
   Server validation is even broader (image/* or GIF by extension). Do not tell
   clients every such image will be analyzed: several analyzers handle only PNG/JPEG.
4. ZIP is mentioned for accountant statements and tax returns, but shared upload
   rules reject it; neither slot has a confirmed ZIP analysis workflow.
5. Old .doc is offered in several pickers but not reliably parsed. Request DOCX/PDF.
6. Material Contracts: portal accepts Excel; analyzer is PDF-oriented.
7. LOI comparison: DOCX picker option lacks DOCX extraction in the analysis route.
8. Occupancy: advisor main picker offers PDF but shared main-slot upload rejects
   it. Binary supporting files are decoded as text in the analysis route.
9. Pricing by Vertical: advisor pricing picker offers DOCX/images, but shared
   pricing_schedule upload only accepts PDF/CSV/Excel.
10. Competitive Pricing's mapped uploads, Owner & GM's mapped supporting files,
    and Facility Review's inspection/citation slots are not all automatically read
    by their current initial-analysis routes. Supply the actual run inputs above.
11. Legal Reports has advisor-only UCC and registered-agent requirements that
    are not represented by dedicated client portal checklist slots.
12. Meeting Notes is a legacy catalog entry with its dedicated route absent.
13. Shared upload validation often trusts MIME OR extension. Listed formats are
    intended UI options, not evidence of file-content integrity or a live test.
